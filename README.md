# 2D Gravitational Poisson Solver & Astrophysical Jeans Collapse

A 2D computational astrophysics simulation modeling the gravitational potential, field vector dynamics, and Virial stability of self-gravitating interstellar gas clouds in Python.

---

## 1. Physical Foundations

### The Gravitational Poisson Equation
In Newtonian gravity, the gravitational potential $\Phi(x, y)$ generated by a continuous mass density distribution $\rho(x, y)$ satisfies the Poisson equation:

$$\nabla^2 \Phi = \frac{\partial^2 \Phi}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2 \Phi}{\partial y^2} = 4\pi G \rho$$

where $G$ is the gravitational constant.

### Mass Density Distribution
The molecular cloud core is modeled as an axisymmetric Gaussian mass profile on a domain of side length $L$:

$$\rho(r) = \frac{M}{2\pi \sigma^2} \exp\left(-\frac{r^2}{2\sigma^2}\right)$$

where $r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$, $M$ is the total integrated gas mass, and $\sigma = 0.15L$ defines the characteristic core radius.

---

## 2. Numerical Methodology

### Finite-Difference Discretization & Iterative Relaxation
The domain is discretized onto an $N \times N$ uniform mesh with grid spacing $h = L / (N - 1)$. Using a standard 5-point central-difference stencil:

$$\frac{\Phi_{i+1, j} - 2\Phi_{i, j} + \Phi_{i-1, j}}{h^2} + \frac{\Phi_{i, j+1} - 2\Phi_{i, j} + \Phi_{i, j-1}}{h^2} = 4\pi G \rho_{i, j}$$

Rearranging yields the iterative Gauss-Seidel update scheme:

$$\Phi_{i, j}^{(k+1)} = \frac{1}{4} \left( \Phi_{i+1, j}^{(k)} + \Phi_{i-1, j}^{(k+1)} + \Phi_{i, j+1}^{(k)} + \Phi_{i, j-1}^{(k+1)} - 4\pi G h^2 \rho_{i, j} \right)$$

* **Boundary Conditions:** Dirichlet boundary conditions ($\Phi = 0$ on all domain boundaries $\partial\Omega$).
* **Convergence Criterion:** Iterations proceed until the maximum residual drops below tolerance $\epsilon = 10^{-5}$:
  
  $$\max_{i, j} \left| \Phi_{i, j}^{(k+1)} - \Phi_{i, j}^{(k)} \right| < \text{tol}$$

### Acceleration Field Extraction
The local gravitational acceleration vector field $\vec{g}(x, y)$ is computed via numerical spatial gradients:

$$\vec{g} = -\nabla\Phi = \left( -\frac{\partial\Phi}{\partial x}, -\frac{\partial\Phi}{\partial y} \right)$$

---

## 3. Energetics & Virial Stability Analysis

### Dual Formulations of Gravitational Binding Energy
The total gravitational potential energy $W$ is calculated and cross-verified using two independent physical formulations:

1. **Matter-Potential Formulation:**
   $$W_{\text{matter}} = \frac{1}{2} \iint \rho(x, y) \Phi(x, y) \, dx\,dy \approx \frac{1}{2} h^2 \sum_{i, j} \rho_{i, j} \Phi_{i, j}$$

2. **Field-Energy Formulation:**
   $$W_{\text{field}} = -\frac{1}{8\pi G} \iint |\vec{g}|^2 \, dx\,dy \approx -\frac{1}{8\pi G} h^2 \sum_{i, j} \left( g_{x, i, j}^2 + g_{y, i, j}^2 \right)$$

### Virial Theorem & Jeans Collapse Criterion
For an isothermal gas cloud with sound speed $c_s$, the internal thermal kinetic energy is:

$$K_{\text{th}} = \frac{3}{2} M c_s^2$$

The critical Jeans sound speed $c_{s, \text{crit}}$ above which thermal gas pressure halts gravitational collapse is derived from the Virial equilibrium condition ($|W| = 2K_{\text{th}}$):

$$c_{s, \text{crit}} = \sqrt{\frac{|W|}{3M}}$$

* **Gravitational Collapse ($|W| > 2K_{\text{th}}$):** Self-gravity overwhelms thermal pressure. The gas core undergoes Jeans collapse, modeled as contraction into a dense protostellar core ($\sigma_{\text{star}} = 0.2\sigma$).
* **Thermal Dispersal ($|W| < 2K_{\text{th}}$):** Thermal pressure dominates. The cloud expands and disperses into the ambient interstellar medium (ISM) ($\sigma_{\text{disp}} = 2.5\sigma$).
